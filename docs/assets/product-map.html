Core · control desk

mok-tua API & CLI

The conductor. Tracks shots, picks engines, dry-runs safely, and keeps private data off the public cloud by default.

Writing helper

Headroom (local LLM gateway)

Expands a rough idea into structured shot lists using your home language models — not a chat bot on the internet.

Picture factory

ComfyUI (storyboards & video)

Open node-based image/video engine. Mac Studio for stills; big GPU tower for longer video when you need it.

Director UI

Director’s Console

Human-friendly control surface for launches and creative sessions — peer of mok-tua, not a replacement for it.

Face & body

FaceFusion · FreeMoCap · LivePortrait

Swap / polish faces, capture body motion, and animate portraits when a shot needs a person who looks consistent.

Sound

ACE-Step · TTS-Story · voice zoo

Music beds and spoken lines for training clips and music-video style pieces — local first.

Optional cloud stills

Grok Imagine · Nano Banana

ai-data pool + bees dashboard
