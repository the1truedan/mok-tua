the pitch, short version

Four things it actually does

>_

Script to storyboard

Feed it a script or sides. It comes back with shot-by-shot stills, ready for review before anything renders.

▶

Multi-model video

Same pipeline, several open video models underneath — swap engines without rewriting the shot.

☰

Best-take curation

Generate a shot more than once, mark the take that worked, assemble picks into one ordered cut.

◈

Runs on what you own

No render farm required — one GPU box is enough; a LAN full of them scales further.
